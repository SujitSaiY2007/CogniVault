# Demo Plan

Target duration: approximately 6–8 minutes, always within the hackathon's 3–10 minute requirement.

## Sequence

1. **Problem** — Generic AI tutors can answer, but may not prove where the course supports the answer or adapt reliably to the learner.
2. **Ingest** — Load representative Mathematics/Science material.
3. **Knowledge Evidence** — Show source-linked evidence and course structure.
4. **Tutor** — Ask a supported question and open its exact evidence.
5. **Unsupported Query** — Ask an off-material question and demonstrate abstention/flagging.
6. **Assessment** — Take an adaptive question selected for the learner.
7. **Feedback** — Show verified correctness feedback linked to source evidence.
8. **Learner Twin** — Show changed topic mastery and likely misconception.
9. **Recommendation** — Show targeted next learning action.
10. **Evaluation** — Show real benchmark results.
11. **Architecture** — Briefly explain the evidence → learner-state feedback loop.

No demo screen may display fabricated metrics or pretend functionality.
